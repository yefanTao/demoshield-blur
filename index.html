STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Questions skeptical engineers ask first.

Why not just use BlurWeb ($67) or a free blur extension?

Two concrete reasons: price + permissions. BlurWeb charges $67+ upfront with no free tier and relies on manual clicking every session. Meanwhile, many free Chrome blur extensions request <all_urls> host permissions, giving them permanent read/write access to every website you visit.

DemoShield Blur gives you unlimited manual click-to-blur free forever, adds 1-click local regex auto-masking for $9 one-time, and is structurally incapable of running in the background because host_permissions does not exist in manifest.json.

Does Auto-Mask send my page text or secrets to an AI server?

No — 0 bytes ever leave your browser. All detection runs via deterministic JavaScript regular expressions and Luhn card validation inside extension/rules.js. Our automated audit test (tests/manifest.test.mjs) fails if fetch(), XMLHttpRequest, WebSocket, or any analytics script is introduced into the extension.

Will blurring break my React, Next.js, or Stripe dashboard state?

No. Manual clicks, form inputs, and leaf dashboard cells are masked strictly via CSS attributes (data-demoshield-mask) without modifying input.value or component state. Mixed prose text nodes wrap only matched substrings in lightweight <ds-mask> tags and cleanly unwrap via normalize() when you click Unblur All.

How do per-origin saved rules work without <all_urls>?

By design, Chrome only grants activeTab access when you explicitly invoke the extension (click toolbar icon, press Alt+Shift+B, or right-click). The instant you activate DemoShield on a tab, it looks up location.origin in local chrome.storage.local and re-applies your saved CSS selectors and auto-mask toggles in <10ms.

How does the $9 Pro offline license key work?

After purchase, you receive a lifetime key formatted as DSPRO-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX. Pasting it into the extension popup verifies its checksum mathematically in rules.js — no login account, no subscription renewal, and no license server phone-home.
